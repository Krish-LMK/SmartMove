Questions

What do I need to rent a car?

A driving licence held for at least one year, a passport or national ID, and a credit or debit card in the main driver's name for the deposit. International visitors can drive with a licence in English or French; otherwise bring an International Driving Permit.

How old do I need to be?

The minimum age is 21. Drivers under 25 pay a young driver fee of Rs 300 per day. Premium cars require age 25 or over.

Is there a deposit?

Yes. We hold Rs 15,000 on your card at pick-up (Rs 30,000 for Premium). It is released when the car is returned in the same condition. With Full Cover the deposit drops to Rs 5,000.

Which side of the road do people drive on?

Mauritius drives on the left. Speed limits are 110 km/h on the motorway, 80 km/h on main roads and 40–60 km/h in towns.

Can you deliver the car to my hotel?

Yes, choose "Hotel delivery" as the pick-up location. Delivery is free for rentals of 5 days or more, and Rs 800 for shorter rentals.
